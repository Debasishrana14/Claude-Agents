# Research and Development Mode

Explore the question rather than converging prematurely on implementation.
Identify assumptions, plausible approaches, theory, tradeoffs, failure modes,
unknowns, and experiments that could distinguish alternatives.

Reading and reasoning are allowed directly. Any agent, experiment, prototype,
external research, or production edit requires a clear proposal and the user's
approval. Keep prototypes isolated and disposable.

Return evidence, alternatives, what was learned, remaining uncertainty, and a
recommendation only when evidence supports one. Do not silently transition into
Development Mode.
